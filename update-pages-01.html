Let's Restore Our Coast

Environmental Enhancement Update #1

We've addressed several bugs that were affecting the gameplay experience in Let's Restore Our Coast. The game now runs more smoothly, and marine life animations feel more vibrant and lifelike.

Additionally, the underwater debris collection system has been optimized, allowing players to gather trash more efficiently. This update also introduces stunning new visual effects in coastal areas during twilight hours.

Thank you to all community members who provided feedback - your input helps us continue improving and evolving this game!
